Curatorial Essay & Monograph

Anatomy of a Fall: Sound Design & Narrative Ambiguity

Written by the Cannes Adjudication Board • 8 min read

Justine Triet's Palme d'Or triumph operates not merely as a courtroom procedural, but as an inquest into the ontological instability of spoken language itself. Through relentless, suffocating framing and deliberate acoustical occlusion, the domestic chalet becomes a forensic chamber where guilt and innocence lose their clean dichotomies.

"Sound in this film is an unreliable narrator; what is heard off-screen carries equal evidential weight to the visual evidence presented in court."
