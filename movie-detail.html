menu_book Curatorial Overview

The Architecture of Time, Gravity, and Human Devotion

In Earth’s twilight era, catastrophic dust blights consume planetary agriculture, threatening human survival. Cooper, an ex-NASA pilot turned dust farmer, stumbles into a secretive clandestine facility orchestrated by Professor Brand. Humanity’s remaining beacon relies on an inexplicable gravitational anomaly near Saturn: a traversable wormhole leading to a distant galaxy holding three candidate habitable worlds orbiting a titanic black hole named Gargantua.

Driven by love across cosmic chasms and anchored by relentless relativity equations, Cooper and Dr. Amelia Brand embark on the Endurance mission—leaving children and eras behind to traverse quantum horizons where one hour on an ocean surface equates to seven standard years of earthly extinction.
